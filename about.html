02 / Who we are

Industrial supply With a practical
approach

Relyco Industrial is a B2B industrial material supplier and sourcing partner based in Saudi Arabia, supporting businesses across industrial, commercial, construction, and infrastructure environments.

Our scope includes industrial materials, tools, equipment, consumables, and related service solutions. We work to understand each requirement, consider the relevant specifications, quantities, and delivery needs, and identify suitable sourcing or service options.

Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, we focus on the specific needs of each enquiry — helping businesses move forward with a clearer understanding of their available options.

Our aim is to make industrial sourcing and service coordination more straightforward, practical, and responsive to business requirements.
